PRIVATE PRODUCTIVITY TOOL

Rental Monitoring App

A private, read-only email monitoring tool used by its owner to identify rental-property updates and outstanding follow-ups.

What it does

  • Reads relevant Gmail messages authorized by the account owner.
  • Produces concise summaries of rental-property updates.
  • Does not sell user data or use it for advertising.

Access and control

Access is limited to the Google account owner. Authorization can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings.
